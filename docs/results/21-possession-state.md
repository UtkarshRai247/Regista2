## 5. Problems and surprises
- The zero in the 80-100/beyond-line/teammate-near cell is an exact zero
  (0 of 2,602 rows scored within 10 actions for the possessing team),
  verified directly against the raw label sum, not a rounding artifact of a
  small sample — the cell is not small (n=2,602).
- Conceding, not just scoring, moves in the wrong direction for the fix's
  premise: in every band, "beyond the line + teammate near" concedes more
  often than "beyond the line + none" (e.g. 100-120: 0.60%→2.28%, a
  roughly fourfold rise). If a nearby teammate reliably signalled a
  genuine, controlled advance, conceding should fall or stay flat, not
  rise this sharply, in the same rows where scoring also failed to rise.
- The `T2` reversal-in-sign story used to justify this task's premise (that
  "beyond the line" being scored as safer is a data-representation defect,
  not a genuine finding) is not contradicted by these numbers — "beyond +
  teammate near" is not obviously less safe than "beyond + none" for
  conceding in the 60-80 band (0.15% vs 0.04%) either. This suggests the
  defect Task 21 targeted may be real, but `teammates_within_5u`/nearest-
  teammate-distance may not be the feature that captures it — a genuine
  line-breaking pass to a teammate in space is not the same state as a
  teammate happening to be within 5 yards of wherever the ball ends up
  (which includes crosses, cutbacks, and defensive teammates who tracked
  back beyond their own line). That distinction is exactly the kind of
  methodology judgment this project's rules reserve for the research lead,
  so it is raised as a question in Section 6, not adopted as a conclusion
  here.
